AI-assisted reply routing and follow-up

I built a workflow that used rules and the OpenAI API to classify campaign replies. It notified Sales when prospects expressed interest, applied opt-outs, and scheduled follow-up for people who asked to reconnect later.

Illustrative replies

“Please check back in three months.”
Saved the timeframe and scheduled a personalized follow-up.
“Please check back sometime later.”
Asked when to reconnect or when the prospect’s contract ended. If they didn’t answer that question, the workflow followed up six months later.
“Please remove me from your list.”
Applied the opt-out in the campaign system.
How the workflow handled replies

Zapier picked up replies sent to a Sales alias in Gmail. Straightforward replies could be handled by rules. When the intent was unclear, a reply could get another pass or go to manual review.

When a prospect gave a timeframe, the workflow saved it and any reason they shared in custom fields in Salesforce Account Engagement. It added eligible contacts to a dynamic list for personalized follow-up in Engagement Studio. When someone declined, the workflow stopped follow-up for that campaign. A salesperson handled interested replies.
