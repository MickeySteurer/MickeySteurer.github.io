Prospect replies and personalized follow-up

“Try me again next quarter” needed to become more than a note in an inbox. I built a workflow that captured when and why a prospect wanted to reconnect, then used that context for personalized outreach at the right time.

How replies were handled
  1. Gmail replyReceived by a sales alias
  2. ZapierPasses the reply for classification
  3. OpenAI APIIdentifies response intent

Selected response paths

Interested

Notify Sales so a salesperson can respond.

Not interested

Stop further emails in the current campaign.

Do not contact

Update contact preferences and suppression records to stop further outreach.

Deferred interest

Turn a request to reconnect later into a scheduled, personalized follow-up.

  1. Extract the timing and reason

    OpenAI’s API identified when the prospect wanted to hear from us and the context behind the request.

  2. Save the context

    Custom fields in Salesforce Account Engagement stored the requested timing and reason.

  3. Schedule the follow-up

    The contact entered the appropriate Engagement Studio workflow. The email referenced their earlier request.

  4. Route interested replies

    A salesperson handled the response and continued the conversation.

03

Invoice intake and budget tracking

I built a workflow that extracted invoice details into Google Sheets, removed duplicates, and updated a working budget view. Changes reached the tracked budget only after I reviewed and confirmed them.

See how invoices reached the budget
  1. Extract invoice details

    Uploading an invoice to Google Drive started the workflow and added its details to an invoice sheet.

  2. Remove duplicates and organize files

    The workflow removed duplicate files and entries, then moved processed invoices into the processed folder.

  3. Update the working budget

    Incoming invoice data updated a temporary budget view and its charts.

  4. Review and confirm

    I checked the invoices and confirmed changes before the workflow updated the tracked budget.

  5. Update the tracked budget

    After confirmation, the workflow automatically updated the tracked budget.

Alongside the budget, I tracked invoice payment status through email.

04

Territory-based sales assignment

I built a workflow that matched prospect locations with company facilities and assigned the corresponding sales contact in Account Engagement. Distant prospects and ambiguous matches were flagged for review.

See how assignment worked
  1. Import prospect locations

    Regional exports from ZoomInfo and other sources supplied the location data.

  2. Compare company facilities

    Google Apps Script compared prospect locations with facility locations to identify a relevant match.

  3. Assign the sales contact

    The workflow assigned the relevant sales alias in Account Engagement.

  4. Flag exceptions

    Distant prospects and unclear matches between facilities were flagged for review.
