01

Sales replies and personalized follow-up

A campaign reply could mean “let’s talk,” “not interested,” or “try me later.” I connected reply classification to the right next step, including personalized outreach when someone asked to reconnect.

Explore the four response paths

Replies to sales aliases arrived in Gmail. Zapier passed them to OpenAI’s API for classification, then the workflow branched by intent.

Positive intent

Add or restore Salesforce and Pardot records where needed, then notify Sales. Contacts were rotated out to manage contact costs, so interested respondents sometimes needed to be added back.

Salesperson writes the reply.

Negative intent

Exclude the contact from further emails in that campaign.

Current campaign stops for that contact.

Do not contact

Update mailability fields and the do-not-contact list to stop further outreach.

Contact is excluded from outreach.

Deferred interest

Extract the requested timing and reason with OpenAI’s API. Custom fields I created in Pardot stored that context for a personalized email at the requested time.

Scheduled email is automated; Sales personally handles interested replies.

Engagement Studio supported the connected follow-up workflow.

02

Invoice intake and budget tracking

Dropping an invoice into Drive started the intake process. I connected extraction, duplicate removal, and budget tracking so incoming costs were visible while confirmed figures stayed separate.

Follow an invoice through the workflow
  1. Upload to Google Drive

    The invoice was automatically scanned and its details extracted into an invoice sheet.

  2. Remove duplicates and organize files

    The workflow removed duplicate files and entries, then moved processed invoices into the processed folder.

  3. Update the temporary budget

    The invoice sheet fed a temporary budget with visualizations that changed as invoices came in.

  4. Confirm the changes

    A person reviewed and confirmed the changes before they could reach the tracked budget.

  5. Save to the tracked budget

    After confirmation, the workflow automatically updated the stable tracked budget.

03

Territory-based sales assignment

Combining facility-by-facility contact exports made sales assignment cumbersome. I built location-based routing to connect prospects with a relevant facility and sales alias.

See how prospects were assigned
  1. Start with prospect locations

    ZoomInfo and other sources supplied contact data. The earlier process involved combining multiple exports for individual facilities.

  2. Compare nearby facilities

    Google Apps Script compared prospect locations with company facilities to identify a relevant match.

  3. Assign the sales alias in Pardot

    The assignment connected prospects with Sales serving the relevant area and rates.

  4. Flag exceptions

    Distant prospects and ambiguous matches between facilities were flagged for attention.

04

Creative file organization

Useful creative work is easier to reuse when you can find it. Working with our designer, I built a naming workflow that used project emails to make a Drive asset library easier to search.

See how context became file names
  1. Work with the designer

    We used the library’s naming conventions and the designer’s project correspondence, with their consent.

  2. Read the project context

    Email language, dates, and project types helped identify what each asset was for. Consistent wording in recurring requests made that context useful.

  3. Apply useful names in Drive

    Google Apps Script used that context to name files, making existing assets easier to retrieve and reuse.
