AI-assisted marketing operations

Prospect reply routing and deferred follow-up

I built a workflow that interpreted campaign replies and routed them appropriately: notify Sales of positive interest, stop unsuitable follow-up, honor opt-out requests, or retain a prospect's requested timing and reason for later contact.

It connected Gmail, Zapier, OpenAI API logic, Salesforce Account Engagement fields, and Engagement Studio follow-up. Ambiguous cases could be reviewed rather than forced into an automated decision.

Illustrative reply · Not an actual prospect message

Response paths

Interested

Notify Sales so a salesperson can respond.

Not interested

Stop further emails in the current campaign.

Do not contact

Update preferences and suppression records to stop outreach.

Deferred interest

Save the time and reason supplied by the prospect and connect the contact to the appropriate personalized follow-up.

  1. Identify timing and context

    Extract the requested follow-up time and the reason, when provided.

  2. Save relevant fields

    Use custom Account Engagement fields to store timing and context.

  3. Enroll for follow-up

    Place eligible contacts into the appropriate dynamic list and Engagement Studio workflow.

  4. Hand interested replies to Sales

    A salesperson handles the conversation.
