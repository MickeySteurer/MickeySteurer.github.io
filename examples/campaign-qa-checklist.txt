CAMPAIGN QA CHECKLIST
Michael Steurer | Portfolio example

Illustrative checklist based on approved work scope; not a historical campaign record.

1. Audience: review the selected list and opt-out logic.
2. Email: test links and check tracking parameters.
3. Landing page: check copy, form behavior, and completion actions.
4. Salesforce: validate lead source, campaign membership, and routing.
5. Follow-up: check who receives responses and owns the next step.
6. After launch: review responses, reporting inputs, and campaign cleanup.
