Website analytics and your choices

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic and improve its content. Measurements include page visits, resume-link clicks, and openings of project explanations. A link click does not confirm a completed download or identify a recruiter.

There is no automatic consent pop-up. By default, Google's regional settings permit analytics cookies for visitors in the United States and deny analytics cookies elsewhere. Outside the United States, Google can still receive cookie-free measurements. Cookie-free measurement is not the same as disabling analytics.

Google may process page URLs, browser and device information, approximate location derived from the request, and, where enabled, analytics cookie identifiers. Advertising personalization, advertising storage, and Google signals are disabled.

Select Privacy in the bottom-right corner to change your preferences. Disable analytics stops further Google Analytics collection in this browser, including cookie-free measurements, and removes this site's analytics cookies. Allow cookies enables analytics cookies regardless of region. Your explicit choice is remembered in local storage; previous choices are respected. Browsers reporting Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track have analytics disabled. These preferences do not affect browsing or resume access.

Analytics reports are available to the site owner through Google Analytics. Optional account-level data sharing with Google is disabled. Read how Google processes information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
